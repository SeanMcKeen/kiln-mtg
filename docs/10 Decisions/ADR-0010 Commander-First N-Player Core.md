# ADR-0010: Commander-First, N-Player Core

## Context
The headline use case is cEDH 4-player pods. All formats are wanted eventually.

## Decision
- The engine is **N-player from day one**. No two-player assumptions anywhere.
- **Commander (and its multiplayer rules) is the first complete format** (M6), ahead of 1v1 constructed, 2HG and Limited (M12).
- The first curated card pool is cEDH staples ([[Curated cEDH Core Pool]]).
- The team concept exists in the model from the start, so 2HG is possible later without rework.

## Consequences
APNAP, leaving the game, and attacking multiple players move early in the roadmap. The test decks are cEDH-style.
